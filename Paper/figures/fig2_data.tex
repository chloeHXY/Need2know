% --- (2) Generator --------------------------------------------------------

\node[bigstage,below=0.55cm of S3,fill=blue!10,

      (S4) {%

\caption{\textbf{Data construction.} Our benchmark is anchored by a
\emph{reverse-synthesis} loop in which a structured sampler proposes
$(\textcolor{\colE}{\mathcal{E}},\textcolor{\colN}{\mathcal{N}})$, a
generator embeds them into a fluent prompt, and a heterogeneous judge
must re-extract the same partition before a sample is admitted; a
two-stage token/embedding matcher gates the loop, and rejected samples
trigger resampling. A WildChat-anchored OOD probe (Aux-A) and a
medical over-removal stress set (Aux-B) are layered on top of the
main loop to verify generalisation under the same
$(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{N})$ schema.}
