\caption{\textbf{Method overview.}
\textbf{(A) Training.} For each query $x$ with annotated
$(\textcolor{\colE}{\mathcal{E}},\textcolor{\colN}{\mathcal{N}})$,
$\pi_\theta$ produces $G$ rollouts; an $\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{N}$
recogniser computes a leak flag (token-coverage $\lor$ BERT precision)
and an essential-recall $\rho$. The reward treats privacy as a
\emph{hard floor} ($R{=}{-1}$ on any leak) and otherwise grades the
rollout by $\rho^{\gamma}\!\cdot\!L_{\text{brev}}$; GRPO uses the
group-relative advantage to update $\pi_\theta$.
\textbf{(B) Inference.} The same frozen $\pi_\theta$ rewrites
$x\!\to\!y$ on-device; only $y$ crosses the trust boundary to the
remote LLM, whose retrieved evidence is recombined with the
on-device-retained original $x$ by a frozen local aggregator to
produce the final answer.}
